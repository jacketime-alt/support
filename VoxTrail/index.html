What VoxTrail is

VoxTrail is a local-first all-day life recorder for iPhone and Apple Watch. It records your day, transcribes audio on device, separates speakers, indexes everything for semantic search, maps where the day happened, and composes an optional private AI review. Audio, transcripts, place data, and reviews stay on your devices. VoxTrail is not a social network or a cloud journal: there is no feed, follow, comment, public profile, or user-to-user messaging.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What is VoxTrail?
VoxTrail is a local-first, all-day life recorder for iPhone and Apple Watch. It records your day, transcribes the audio on device, indexes it for semantic search, and composes a private AI review of what happened — what was said, what was decided, and what to remember.
How does recording work?
Start capture from your Apple Watch or iPhone and let it run through your day. Marks, pauses, and interruptions are recorded as they happen, and quiet hours let you stop capture when you want silence. Watch recordings transfer to your iPhone over Watch Connectivity.
How does transcription work?
Transcription runs on device with local speech models. No cloud transcription, no audio uploads. Your audio becomes text on your device, and transcription and search work even without an internet connection.
What are speaker labels?
Voiceprint recognition separates who said what, so a conversation reads as a conversation. You can rename speakers as you go, and the labels stay consistent across the day.
What is semantic search?
Instead of matching exact words, semantic search finds moments by meaning across your whole archive. Ask about a topic or idea, and results link straight back to the source audio.
What's the difference between Free and Premium?
The free tier includes unlimited recording and 60 minutes of transcription per day, plus playback, search, and export — but no AI reviews. Premium unlocks unlimited transcription, AI hour/day/week/month reviews, chat, follow-ups, and daily improvement (Apple Intelligence or your own key).
What do the three paid plans include?
All three plans — Monthly, Yearly, and Lifetime — unlock exactly the same features: unlimited transcription, AI hour/day/week/month reviews, chat, follow-ups, daily improvement, and the completed-follow-up history (Apple Intelligence or your own key). They differ only in how you pay. Monthly and Yearly are auto-renewing subscriptions with a 7-day free trial; Lifetime is a one-time purchase.
How do I cancel my subscription?
Subscriptions are billed and managed through Apple. Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period in the App Store or in Settings. You can also restore a purchase anytime from Settings.
Do I need an AI key?
No. Recording, transcription, and search need no AI provider. Day review and chat are optional. You can use Apple Intelligence, or bring your own key for a provider you already use. There is no VoxTrail-operated AI service.
Which AI providers are supported?
Bring-your-own-key presets include DeepSeek, OpenAI, Claude, Gemini, Grok, MiniMax, Kimi, GLM, and Qwen. You can also enter a custom endpoint and model. Your key is stored in the iOS Keychain, and requests go directly from your device to the provider you configure.
How do I export my data?
Your archive is yours. Export transcripts, audio packages, or review cards through the system share sheet. No account, no lock-in.
What Health data does VoxTrail read?
Optionally and read-only: heart rate, heart rate variability (SDNN), resting heart rate, steps, sleep, and workouts — and only the types you select. Samples stay on your device and appear as non-clinical context beside your timeline. VoxTrail does not write Health data and is not a medical device.
Does VoxTrail work offline?
Yes. Core recording, on-device transcription, and local search work offline. The only network calls happen when you use an optional feature: AI review or chat (Apple Intelligence or your own key), or a place-name lookup via Apple's geocoding service.
Is my data private?
Yes. Audio, transcripts, search indexes, and reviews stay on your devices. There is no account, no cloud archive, no iCloud sync, no ads, and no tracking. Your archive is yours.
How do follow-ups work, and where do completed items go?
VoxTrail surfaces intentions and open loops from your recorded hours as follow-ups. Dated Calendar or Reminders candidates wait for your confirmation; nothing is written until you confirm each item. When you mark a follow-up 已完成, it moves to the 已完成 / 已关闭 history on the Follow-ups page. Rows completed directly offer 回看 and 重新打开; rows closed by a same-intent Calendar/Reminders decision are labeled 已关闭.
What is Daily Improvement?
A review can produce one small commitment. Daily Improvement groups commitments into due, upcoming, and history; you check in as done, not done, or dropped. There are no streaks, and the check-in is reversible.
What is Place Trail?
Place Trail is an optional map of your day, built from location sampled only while recording and, when available, Health workout routes. It shows stops, paths, and footprints; place names come from Apple geocoding. Location is off unless you enable it, and exports do not include coordinates or a map by default.
What are weekly and monthly reviews?
VoxTrail rolls reviewed days into a weekly or monthly review, so patterns are visible without rereading every entry. The period entry shows how many recorded days already have reviews and what is still missing.
Which spoken languages are supported?
The built-in on-device engine supports Chinese, English, Japanese, Korean, Cantonese, and Auto. The optional High Accuracy engine covers about 100 languages. On iOS 26+, Apple's system speech model is another choice when it is installed and the locale is available.
Is VoxTrail a social app?
No. VoxTrail has no feed, follow, comment, like, public profile, or user-to-user messaging. Sharing is an explicit handoff to the system share sheet. The app is single-user and local-first.
