Everything You Can Do

A local-first recorder that turns your day into something you can search, map, and reflect on.

🎙️

All-Day Recording

Start capture from your Apple Watch or iPhone and let it run through your day. Marks, pauses, and interruptions are recorded as they happen. Quiet hours let you stop capture when you want silence.

🔒

On-Device Transcription

Your audio becomes text on your device, powered by local speech models. No cloud transcription, no audio uploads. Transcription and search work even without an internet connection.

👥

Speaker Labels

Voiceprint recognition separates who said what, so a conversation reads as a conversation. Rename speakers as you go and watch the transcript stay organized across the whole day.

🔍

Semantic Search

Find any moment from what you actually said. Search by meaning, not just keywords, across your whole archive. Results link straight back to the source audio.

🌙

Day Review

End the day with an AI summary: what happened, what was decided, and what to remember. Hour summaries and remembered facts build a journal that writes itself.

💬

Chat About Your Day

Ask questions about your day in a private AI chat, grounded in your own transcripts. Optional, and off until you enable it.

✅

Follow-ups That Stay Findable

VoxTrail surfaces intentions and open loops from your day. Nothing is written to Calendar or Reminders until you confirm each item. Completed follow-ups remain in a history list, with undo and optional reopen.

📈

Daily Improvement

Turn a review into one small commitment, then check in when it comes due: done, not done, or dropped. No streaks and no guilt.

🗺️

Place Trail

See where your day happened on a map, built from location sampled only while recording, plus workout routes from Health. Location is off unless you enable it.

📅

Week & Month Reviews

AI rolls reviewed days into weekly and monthly patterns, so you can zoom out without rereading every entry.

⌚

Apple Watch

The watch is a recorder first. Capture on your wrist, transfer to your iPhone, and see your day from the watch — with complications to start recording at a glance.

❤️

Health & Context

Optional read-only health context — heart rate, heart rate variability, steps, sleep, and workouts — sits beside your timeline as non-clinical context for your own review.

🗝️

Bring Your Own Key

Use your own API key with DeepSeek, OpenAI, Claude, Gemini, Grok, MiniMax, Kimi, GLM, Qwen, or a custom endpoint. The key stays in your Keychain and requests go directly from your device to the provider — never through a VoxTrail server.

🍎

Apple Intelligence

Prefer Apple? Day review and chat can run through Apple Intelligence instead. Only the text needed for that request is sent, under Apple's Privacy Policy. Audio is never sent.

📤

Export & Share

Your archive is yours. Export what you choose through the system share sheet — transcripts, audio packages, or a review card. No account, no lock-in.

🛡️

Private by Default

Recording, transcription, and search stay on your devices. Optional AI sends only the text you choose, directly to a provider you pick. No account, no cloud archive, no iCloud sync, no ads, no tracking. Your recordings and the text you create remain yours.
