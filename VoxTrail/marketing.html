A day, end to end

Three moments. One loop.

Capture on the wrist, write on the phone, read it back at night. Most of it happens while the phone is in your pocket.

Step 01 — Capture

The face says REC.

Start from a complication on your Infograph face. The top-right corner reads REC while it records and READY when idle, so you never open the app to check. Marks, pauses, quiet hours, and interruptions are kept as they happen, and a Live Activity tracks the take on your iPhone.

  • No app to open
  • Live Activity
  • Quiet hours

Step 02 — Transcribe

Every conversation, written down.

Speech becomes text on your iPhone with local models — no upload, no account. Timestamps stay attached, the original audio sits one tap away, and voiceprint recognition splits who said what so a conversation reads like a conversation.

  • On-device
  • Speaker labels
  • Tap to replay

Step 03 — Review

Your day, already written.

Hour summaries roll into a day review: what happened, what was decided, what to remember. Week and month reviews zoom out. Open loops become follow-ups you confirm — nothing is written to Calendar or Reminders until you do.

  • Undo and reopen

Questions, answered

The short version.

More detail lives in Help & Support.

What is VoxTrail?

VoxTrail is a local-first all-day life recorder for iPhone and Apple Watch. It records your day, transcribes the audio on device, indexes it for semantic search, maps where the day happened, and composes an optional private AI review. It is not a social network or a cloud journal.

Is my audio private?

Yes. Audio, transcripts, search indexes, and reviews stay on your devices. There is no account, no cloud archive, no iCloud sync, no ads, and no tracking. Optional AI sends only the text for the request you make — to Apple Intelligence or to a provider you configure with your own key. Audio is never sent.

Do I need a subscription to use VoxTrail?

No. The free tier includes unlimited recording, 60 minutes of transcription per day, playback, search, and export. Premium unlocks unlimited transcription, AI hour/day/week/month reviews, chat, follow-ups, and daily improvement.

Does it work offline?

Recording, on-device transcription, and semantic search work without a connection. AI reviews and chat need a connection because they call Apple Intelligence or the provider you configured.

How do follow-ups work?

VoxTrail surfaces intentions and open loops from your recorded hours. Nothing is written to Calendar or Reminders until you confirm each item. Completed items remain in the 已完成 / 已关闭 history; directly completed rows can be reopened, and reopening is undoable.

Which devices are supported?

An iPhone on iOS 18 or later and an Apple Watch on watchOS 11 or later. Apple Intelligence requires a supported device, and the High Accuracy transcription engine is recommended on iPhone 14 Pro or newer.
