1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use the VoxTrail application for iPhone and Apple Watch (the "App"), available on the Apple App Store.

VoxTrail is a local-first recorder. Audio you capture, transcripts, search indexes, day reviews, and remembered facts stay on your iPhone and Apple Watch. We do not operate a cloud archive, and the App does not sync your archive to iCloud or to a VoxTrail server.

Some optional features send limited text or sensor context to Apple or to an AI provider you choose. Those cases are described below. If you do not agree with this policy, do not use the App.

2. Information Stored on Your Devices

Core archive data is stored on your iPhone and, while a recording is in progress or waiting to transfer, on your Apple Watch. Watch recordings move to the iPhone through Apple's Watch Connectivity on your devices. We do not receive that transfer.

  • Audio segments, marks, pause and interruption gaps, and transfer status
  • On-device transcripts, speaker labels you rename, and the local search index
  • Day reviews, hour summaries, and remembered facts you save
  • Optional body and place labels derived on device from data you allow
  • App settings, including language and quiet hours

You can delete audio or days in the App, and you can export a plain (unencrypted) package through the system share sheet. Uninstalling the App removes the local archive from that device. A bring-your-own-key API key, if you save one, is stored in the iOS Keychain on your device and is not sent to us.

3. Purchases

Local Edition is an optional one-time purchase processed by Apple's StoreKit, with a trial period offered in the App. We do not collect or store your payment card details, billing address, or Apple ID password. Apple processes payment under Apple's Privacy Policy. We receive only the purchase status needed to unlock the App and to restore purchases, including Family Sharing where Apple provides it.

4. Permissions

The App asks for a permission only when the related feature needs it. You can deny any of these. Denying a permission limits that feature and does not block features that do not need it.

  • Microphone (Apple Watch, and iPhone when you import or test audio): used to record the audio you start or that the Watch starts under the rules you set. Recording can include voices of other people. You are responsible for recording only where the law allows.
  • Health (optional, read-only): heart rate, heart rate variability (SDNN), resting heart rate, steps, sleep, and workouts, and only the types you select. Samples stay on your device and are used as non-clinical context beside the day. VoxTrail does not write Health data and is not a medical device.
  • Location (optional, while recording): approximate places for the timeline. Place names may be requested from Apple's geocoding service, which can receive coordinates for that lookup. Raw coordinates are not uploaded to us.
  • Calendars and Reminders (optional): used only after you confirm a suggested action. The App does not write an event or reminder without that confirmation.
  • Notifications: requested only when the App is about to tell you that a recording was interrupted or that a review is ready. Onboarding does not ask for notification permission.

5. Optional AI

Recording, on-device transcription, and local search work without an AI provider. Day review and chat are optional.

5.1 Apple Intelligence

If you select Apple Intelligence, the App sends the text needed for that review or chat — transcript excerpts and condensed context such as remembered facts or place and body labels you enabled — to Apple's Foundation Models. Processing may happen on your device or through Apple Intelligence, under Apple's Privacy Policy. We do not receive that text. Audio is not sent for this feature.

5.2 Your own API key

If you select a bring-your-own-key provider, you enter an endpoint, model, and API key. The key stays in the Keychain. A connection check, and later review or chat requests, go directly from your device to the endpoint you configured. We do not operate a relay and we do not receive the key, the prompt, or the reply.

  • What is sent is text: the connection-check prompt, transcript excerpts, and condensed context allowed by your settings. Raw audio is not uploaded for AI.
  • Presets include DeepSeek, OpenAI, Claude, Gemini, Grok, MiniMax, Kimi, GLM, and Qwen. You may also enter a custom endpoint. That provider's privacy policy governs the text it receives, and the provider may charge you.
  • You can delete the saved key in Settings. Local recording, transcription, and search keep working.

6. What We Do Not Do

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not run advertising or third-party analytics SDKs in the App.
  • We do not upload your archive to a VoxTrail server or sync it through iCloud as an archive backup.
  • We do not use Health or location data to diagnose, treat, or predict a medical condition.

This support website is a static page. Opening the privacy policy or terms does not send your archive to us.

7. Your Choices

  • You can review and delete local audio, transcripts, and days in the App, and export what you choose to keep.
  • You can turn off Health, Location, Calendar, and Reminders, and you can delete a saved API key.
  • You can refuse microphone access. The Watch will not record until permission is granted.
  • Uninstalling the App deletes its local data on that device.

Depending on where you live, including under the GDPR or the CCPA, you may have additional rights to access, correct, or delete personal information a business holds about you. Because the archive is on your devices and we do not keep a copy, those requests are carried out in the App. For questions, email us. We will respond within 30 days when the law requires a response.

8. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 where the GDPR applies). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through a feature that reaches us, contact us and we will delete what we hold.

9. Changes

We may update this policy when the App or the law changes. The effective date at the top will change. Continued use of the App after the new date means you accept the updated policy.
