Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Fur Baby Companion.

How does Fur Baby Companion calculate pet age?

The app uses official veterinary guidelines — AVMA for dogs and cats, ARBA for rabbits, ASMVP for small mammals, AAV for birds, and AFA for ferrets — rather than the outdated "1 year = 7 human years" rule. Each species has a unique aging curve calibrated to its life stages. For dogs, the calculation also accounts for size (small/medium/large/giant), which significantly affects aging rate.

Which pet species does Fur Baby Companion support?

Fur Baby Companion supports 9 species: Dog (with 4 size categories: small, medium, large, giant), Cat, Rabbit, Guinea Pig, Hamster, Parrot (with 3 size categories), Ferret, Reptile, and a Custom species option for any pet not listed — you can set your own conversion rate for custom species.

Does the app work offline? Is my data private?

Yes, fully offline. All data is stored locally on your iPhone using Apple SwiftData. No account creation is required. No analytics SDKs, no tracking, no server. Age calculation, memory journaling, wallpaper creation, and widgets all work without internet. The only online feature is the optional AI wallpaper generator, which sends your prompt to your chosen AI provider using your own API key.

Is Fur Baby Companion free?

Fur Baby Companion has a free tier with: up to 2 pets, 5 species, 50 memory records, 5 milestones, 3 wallpaper templates, and lock screen widgets. Pro ($2.99/month or $39.99 lifetime) unlocks: unlimited pets, all 9 species, unlimited records with video support, all 6 wallpaper templates, unlimited milestones, and PDF/Image/JSON export.

How is this different from other pet tracking apps?

Unlike single-purpose pet apps, Fur Baby Companion combines four tools in one: (1) vet-accurate age calculator with official guidelines, (2) rich memory journal with photos, location, mood tracking, and 5 view modes, (3) custom lock screen wallpaper maker with 6 templates, and (4) milestone reminders synced to your calendar. All ad-free, no account required, all data stored locally.

How do I calculate my dog's age in human years?

Download Fur Baby Companion (free on the iOS App Store), add your dog's profile with their birth date and size category (small/medium/large/giant), and the app instantly shows their age in human years based on AVMA canine life stage guidelines — far more accurate than the "multiply by 7" rule. For example, a 2-year-old medium dog is approximately 24 human years.
