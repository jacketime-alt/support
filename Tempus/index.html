Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about My Tempus.

What is My Tempus?

My Tempus is a free life progress tracker for iOS that visualizes your lifespan as an interactive grid of colored cells. Each cell represents a unit of time (day, week, month, or year). You record moments — photos, moods, tags, and locations — directly into cells. It also includes event countdown timers, companion tracking for people and pets, custom data wallpapers, and six Home Screen widget variants.

Is My Tempus free?

Yes. The free tier includes 2 companions, 2 moments per companion, 2 event timers, 3 wallpapers per day, and Small + Lock Screen widgets. Pro unlocks unlimited everything with optional one-time Lifetime purchase ($79.99) — no subscription trap.

Does My Tempus work offline?

Yes. All data is stored locally on your iPhone using Core Data — no account creation, no cloud servers, and no internet connection needed for any core feature. The only network use is the optional AI assistant, which runs only if you turn it on with your own API key.

How is My Tempus different from a journal app?

Journal apps show entries as a text list. My Tempus visualizes your life as a spatial grid — blue cells for time passed, yellow for the present, gray for the future. This spatial representation creates a fundamentally different emotional connection: you see your time, not just read about it.

How the Life Grid Works
