Life Progress Widget

Make time visible. Track life progress for yourself, your family, and your pets — with a beautiful grid, event timers, and a rich timeline. Relive your story through immersive Retrospect recaps and Story mode, dress your lock screen with data wallpapers, and keep it all close with always-on widgets. Every moment counts.

Everything You Can Do

A complete toolkit for visualizing, recording, and sharing the time that matters.

🔲

Life Grid

Visualize any companion's lifespan as a grid of cells — day, week, month, or year. Each cell represents a unit of time. 13 built-in templates cover emotional bonds, personal growth, and life milestones, plus custom templates of your own. Tap any cell to view moments from that day.

📅

Event Timers

Count down to future dates or count up from past ones. Birthdays, anniversaries, trips, deadlines — each with custom icons and colors. Events appear as markers on the Life Grid and can be pinned to your lock screen widget.

📋

Rich Timeline

Record life moments with mood, text, tags, location, and photos. Browse them in five views: List (grouped by date, searchable), Calendar (month grid), Map (geotagged moments), Photos (filterable photo grid), and Statistics (mood charts, top locations, activity trends).

✨

Retrospect Recaps

Your moments, composed into an immersive, auto-playing recap with its own soundtrack. Recaps arrive for weekly and monthly summaries, streak milestones, memorable moment counts, birthdays, and completed countdowns — with count-up stats, mood trends, photo memories, a map of your places, and auto-detected themes. Share any recap as beautiful cards, or revisit them in your Library.

▶️

Story Mode

Play your moments as a full-screen, auto-advancing story with a progress bar and background music. Switch between Highlights and Recent, tap any card to open its detail, and hold to pause. A cinematic way to look back on the days that mattered.

👥

Companions

Companions — track yourself, your partner, kids, parents, and pets all in one app. Give each companion a photo avatar (snap or pick from library, cropped into a circular portrait). Set their birth date and watch their age update in real time. Pets have species-specific lifespans with custom pet types (define any animal with custom icon and lifespan). Each companion gets their own grid, stats, and timeline. Free: 2 companions. Pro: unlimited.

🖼️

Wallpapers

Turn your life grid into a beautiful lock screen or home screen wallpaper. 3 styles (Minimal, Data, Story) across 5 layouts including premium Storywall and Pure. Free tier: 3 wallpapers per day. Pro: unlimited.

🧩

6 Widget Variants

Life Progress (progress bar), Events (countdown display), Grid (mini life grid with labels), Ring (circular gauge), Calendar (heatmap), and Composite (ring + grid + stats). Free: Small + Lock Screen. Pro: all sizes.

📸

Photo Gallery & Viewer

All your moment photos in one place. Browse photos grouped by day, week, month, or year. Filter by tags and companions. The moment editor uses an adaptive image grid — single photos display at natural aspect ratio, multiple photos align at unified row height with width adapting to each photo's proportions. Full-screen viewer with pinch-to-zoom and swipe navigation. Choose between Reference mode or Local Copy storage.

⚡

Smart Recording

The moment editor adapts to you. Frequently used sections auto-expand. Adaptive photo grid preserves aspect ratios — no forced cropping. Event icons support 50+ SF Symbols (6 categories), 80+ emoji (7 categories), or custom photos — all searchable. 8 color choices per timer. Mark events complete to hide them from widgets and keep your list clean.

📤

Moment Sharing

Turn any moment into a beautiful share card with mood, photos, location, tags, companions, and event info. Rendered as a high-resolution image, shared via the system share sheet. Also available from the timeline context menu.

🎨

Personalized & Private

4 themes: System, Light, Dark, and Sepia. Customizable icons and colors for every event and companion. All data stored locally on your device — no accounts, no cloud, no tracking. Your life data is yours alone. An optional AI assistant (off by default, uses your own API key) can turn a sentence into a moment, event, or companion when you want the shortcut.
